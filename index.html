Form M is a mandatory document for importing goods into Nigeria. It is processed through an authorised dealer bank before your goods are shipped. We help you prepare and process it correctly so your import is not delayed.

The Pre-Arrival Assessment Report (PAAR) is issued by the Nigeria Customs Service and is used to assess and clear your cargo. We handle the PAAR process as part of your customs documentation.
