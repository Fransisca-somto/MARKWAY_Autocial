About Us

Your One-Stop Partner for Clearing, Forwarding & Logistics

Regulatory Documentation & Compliance

Mark P N O Unique Way Enterprises (Markway) provides comprehensive clearing, forwarding, import and export logistics services. We assist importers, exporters, manufacturers, traders and businesses with the documentation, regulatory processes, customs clearance and transportation required to move their goods efficiently from origin to final destination.

  • Form M, PAAR, SONCAP and NAFDAC processing.
  • Port and airport customs clearance and cargo release.
  • Haulage, container handling and final delivery.
